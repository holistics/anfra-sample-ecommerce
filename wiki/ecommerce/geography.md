# Geography

- A customer's market is `customers.country`. A session's market is `sessions.country`.
- Regions are `APAC` (Australia, Japan, Singapore, Vietnam), `Europe` (Germany) and `Americas` (United States).
- `markets.code` is the ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code, for matching map shapes.
- UTC offsets in `markets.utc_offset` are fixed: the data ignores daylight saving time.
