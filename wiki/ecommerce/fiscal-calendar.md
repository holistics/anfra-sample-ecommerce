# Fiscal calendar

The fiscal year starts on 1 July and is named after the calendar year it ends in.

| Fiscal period | Months |
| --- | --- |
| FY2027 | July 2026 to June 2027 |
| Q1 | July to September |
| Q2 | October to December |
| Q3 | January to March |
| Q4 | April to June |

`orders.fiscal_year` and `orders.fiscal_quarter` compute these from `ordered_at`. Check which fiscal years the data covers in full before comparing them.
